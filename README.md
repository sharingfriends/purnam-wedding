# Purnima & Shivam — Wedding Invitation Website 💍✨

A luxury, interactive wedding invitation website crafted for **Purnima & Shivam** celebrating their wedding ceremonies in Patiala, Punjab.

**Live Website:** [https://purnam-wedding.vercel.app](https://purnam-wedding.vercel.app)

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Monogram & Royal Cover**: Custom animated "PURNAM" monogram with "Tap to Reveal" unsealing animation.
- **Blessings & Family Lineage**: Structured, royal presentation of ancestral lineages for both the Bride & Groom.
- **Interactive 'Reveal the Date'**:
  - 3 scratch-card gold hearts to reveal the Day (25), Month (Nov), and Year (2026).
  - Real-time countdown timer to the wedding day (25th November 2026).
- **Wedding Itinerary & Events**:
  - **Mehandi**: Monday, 23rd Nov 2026, 5:00 PM @ Vintage Hall, RGMC Club, Patiala
  - **Haldi**: Tuesday, 24th Nov 2026, 10:00 AM @ Pool Side, RGMC Club, Patiala
  - **Sangeet**: Tuesday, 24th Nov 2026, 5:00 PM @ Terrace Lounge, RGMC Club, Patiala
  - **Wedding**: Wednesday, 25th Nov 2026, 10:00 AM @ Bahadurgarh Palace, Fort Patiala
- **Venue Spotlight**: Integrated "Open in Maps" navigation buttons for Google Maps.
- **Dress Code Guide**: Heartfelt note celebrating personal comfort and style.
- **Interactive RSVP**: Sleek response form capturing guest attendance, DJ song requests, and heartfelt wishes.
- **Couple Gallery ("Our Beautiful Moments")**: Swipeable photo carousel with ambient audio support and floating gold particle canvas.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **HTML5 & CSS3** (Vanilla, single-file, zero-dependency)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Interactive scratch canvas, countdown, particle engine, carousel)
- **Hosted on**: [Vercel](https://vercel.com)

---

## 💻 Local Development

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser, or serve locally with any static server:

```bash
npx serve .
```
